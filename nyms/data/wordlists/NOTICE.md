# Word lists

`eff_short_wordlist_2_0.txt`: the Electronic Frontier Foundation's short wordlist 2.0 (1,296 words, unique three-letter prefixes), from https://www.eff.org/dice, licensed CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Downloaded 2026-11-08, unchanged. Used by `nyms.surrogates` to render keyed hashes and random bytes as readable words.
